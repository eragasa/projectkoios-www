Course materials from classes I teach at De La Salle University.

These pages collect lecture notes, references, examples, computational material, and course-specific working notes from my teaching in physics and materials science.

Course index

This is a working index of DLSU course material. Some pages may be empty until I migrate notes, examples, and supporting material into the site.

SOLST01: Solid State Physics Crystal structure, reciprocal space, diffraction, phonons, electrons, band structure, and computational models of solids. SEMPHY01: Semiconductor Physics Semiconductor materials, carrier statistics, transport, junctions, devices, and electronic structure. MTSCI02: Materials Science 2 Thermodynamics, phase diagrams, phase transitions, kinetics, diffusion, and microstructure evolution. MTSCI03: Materials Science 3 Thin films, materials characterization, processing, structure-property relationships, and applied materials systems. PHYS104: Modern Physics Relativity, quantum foundations, atomic models, wave mechanics, nuclei, particles, and the transition from classical to quantum physics. ENGPY1: Engineering Physics I Relativity, quantum foundations, atomic models, wave mechanics, nuclei, particles, and the transition from classical to quantum physics.

How the course material is written

My course notes usually connect standard theory to computation, and materials science concepts to fundamental physics.

I often use lectures to review material students should have seen before and to introduce mathematical tools that are useful for the problem at hand.

The notes may move between derivations, assumptions, numerical examples, code, diagrams, and interpretation. The point is to make the machinery visible enough that students can work with it.
