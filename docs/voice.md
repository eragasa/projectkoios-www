## Tone

The tone should be rigorous, direct, and technical.

Avoid hype. Avoid vague claims about innovation, disruption, or intelligence. Prefer precise language about models, computation, validation, uncertainty, systems, and implementation.

The site can be personal, but it should not become casual to the point of losing technical credibility.

## Preferred Language

Use phrases such as:

- technical outreach
- models, notes, and experiments
- executable thinking
- practical modeling
- mathematical modeling
- computational models
- scientific computing
- inspectable numerical models
- reproducible workflows
- technical essays
- physically grounded models
- systems that can be built and tested
- ideas that can be derived, inspected, run, and improved

## Language to Avoid

Avoid leading with phrases such as:

- AI for science
- next-generation education platform
- revolutionary technology
- democratizing everything
- intelligent systems without qualification
- black-box automation
- no-code physics
- passive income content
- stock tips
- thought leadership

These phrases are either too generic, too inflated, or too far from the technical center of the project.
