## Copyright Position

Project Koios is public because I want people to read it, learn from it, and use it as a reference. It is not public because I am giving away all commercial rights to my work.

My position is straightforward.

I want students, teachers, researchers, and technically curious readers to be able to share Project Koios material for education and learning, provided they give proper attribution. If someone finds a note useful for a class, a discussion, a reading group, or personal study, that is part of the purpose of the site.

At the same time, I may want to turn parts of this work into books, courses, software, paid materials, consulting products, or other commercial projects. I am not placing the entire body of work into the public domain. I am also not giving permission for someone else to extract the material, remove attribution, repackage it, put it behind a paywall, or sell it as their own product.

Unless otherwise stated, the original text, figures, diagrams, notes, essays, course materials, and website content created by Eugene Joseph M. Ragasa are copyright © Eugene Joseph M. Ragasa. Commercial rights are reserved.

Educational sharing is allowed under these conditions:

- The use is for teaching, learning, classroom discussion, academic reference, or personal study.
- Proper attribution is given to Eugene Joseph M. Ragasa and Project Koios.
- The material is not sold, repackaged into a paid product, placed behind a paywall, or used for commercial training without written permission.
- The material is not presented as someone else's work.
- Large-scale republication, substantial modification, translation, compilation, or derivative use requires permission unless a specific page says otherwise.

Short excerpts may be quoted with attribution for education, commentary, criticism, academic discussion, or reference.

Project Koios also depends on notes about what I consider the best reference tools: textbooks, papers, articles, websites, datasets, figures, diagrams, software, and other technical sources. I attempt to provide attribution when necessary. Any copyrighted material from external sources remains the property of the original author, publisher, institution, or copyright owner.

If copyrighted third-party information appears in my notes, it is incidental to teaching, commentary, criticism, reference, or scholarly discussion. I do not claim ownership over third-party copyrighted material. Those rights remain with the original copyright holder.

Code is a separate issue. Reusable code should eventually live in dedicated repositories with explicit licenses. I will likely release reusable code under a standard open-source software license such as MIT or Apache-2.0, depending on the project. Until a separate license is provided, code snippets on Project Koios are for reading, teaching, and instructional reference only. They are not automatically licensed for commercial or production reuse.

In short: share for education, cite the source, do not pretend it is yours, and do not commercialize it without permission. This is not a complicated deal.
